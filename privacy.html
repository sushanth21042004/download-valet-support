Privacy, without fine print.

Download Valet is designed to organize files locally. Your files and filenames are not uploaded to us.

Data we collect

Download Valet does not collect personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, usage telemetry, filenames, file contents, or folder contents.

File access

The app asks you to choose a folder through the standard macOS folder picker. macOS grants sandboxed read-and-write access to that folder so Download Valet can inspect and organize its contents. File classification, duplicate checks, cleanup suggestions, and rules run on your Mac.

Data stored on your Mac

The app may store preferences, custom rules, exclusions, a security-scoped folder bookmark, purchase status, and recent organization history in local app storage. You can remove this data by deleting the app and its container.

Purchases

Download Valet Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the Mac App Store. We do not receive or store your payment-card information. Apple’s own privacy policy governs its processing.

Notifications and Trash

If you enable notifications, they are delivered by macOS. Files you choose to remove are sent to the macOS Trash rather than permanently erased by Download Valet.

Accounts, cloud services, and AI

The app does not require an account and does not use a Download Valet cloud service or AI processing.

Children’s privacy

Because no personal information is collected, Download Valet does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change is released and its effective date will be revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, email astrolab704@gmail.com or use the Support page.
